04 · Clearspace & minimums

Hold a margin equal to the height of the chain's ring around the entire lockup. Don't crowd it — the chain needs to read as two distinct, interlocked shapes.

Minimum size

Digital: never smaller than 120 px wide for the lockup, or 32 px for the mark.

Print

Never smaller than 30 mm wide for the lockup, or 10 mm for the mark.

07 · Do & Don't

A few rules that protect the mark's integrity. Anything else is fair game.

Do Use the lockup as supplied. Two-tone ink + sky blue, on cream or paper.
shopy link
Don't Don't recolour. The sky blue is the brand.
Do Keep the chain rings interlocked — left over at the bottom, blue over at the top.
shopy link
Don't Don't skew, rotate, or distort the lockup.
Do On dark surfaces, use the Clean/Sky 400 variant for legibility.
Don't Don't place the dark wordmark on sky blue — not enough contrast.
