03

Bilingual system

النظام ثنائي اللغة

The Arabic wordmark شوبي لينك is set in Tajawal Black to match Bricolage's weight. The chain mark is shared, unchanged, between both scripts.

shopylink
Latin. Bricolage 800 · reads shopy → link
شوبيلينك
Arabic. Tajawal 900 · reads شوبي ← لينك
shopylink شوبيلينك
Stacked lockup. Latin on top, Arabic below, centred — shared chain.
Reading order. Arabic flows right→left: شوبي sits right, لينك left. The chain stays in the middle.
Direction. In Arabic layouts set dir="rtl"; arrows and the tagline flip to point ← .
Never mix scripts. Keep each script whole — don't interleave Latin and Arabic in one lockup line.

ShopyLink · Brand Guide07 · Voice & tone
07

Voice & tone

الصوت والنبرة

Warm, plain-spoken, quietly confident. We are the friend abroad who handles the boring part so the parcel just arrives.

We sound

Clear over clever. Short sentences. We say what we do — receive, consolidate, deliver — and skip the hype.

We don't sound

Corporate, jargon-heavy, or breathless. No "revolutionary logistics synergy." Just: your stuff, brought home.

نبرتنا بالعربية

واضحة وقريبة من الناس، بلهجة بسيطة ومطمئنة. نقول ما نفعله بالضبط: نستلم، نجمّع، ونوصّل إلى بابك.

Lines we'd write

EN. "You shop the world. We bring it home." · "One address in every market." · "Track it from cart to doorstep."
AR. «تسوّق من كل العالم، ونحن نوصّله لبيتك.» · «عنوان واحد في كل سوق.» · «تابع طردك من السلة حتى الباب.»
