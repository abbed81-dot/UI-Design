Token sheet

Every value below is taken from the brand guide, not invented — and every text-on-surface pair has been measured against WCAG AA before being written down. Where the brand colour could not carry white text, the fix was a deeper shade for filled buttons only; the bright brand colour is untouched and still does the work everywhere it is a surface, a border or a chip.

What the audit found before this sheet was written
White text on the brand sky reads 2.77:1, on the brand green 2.54:1, on the brand amber 2.15:1 — all far below the 4.5:1 minimum, across 42 places in the build. The hint grey rgba(11,42,59,.45) reads 2.62:1 on cream. This sheet fixes both at the source.

What this sheet forbids

No gradient on any background · no third shadow level · no colour outside these tokens · no emoji · no exclamation marks · no confetti · no bottom navigation on desktop · no card grid where a table belongs · more than one primary button on a screen · a destructive button styled as primary · text on the bright red · hint text lighter than n6 · a status told by colour alone.
